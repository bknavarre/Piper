Piper Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UpSwing Entertainment LLC (“UpSwing,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use Piper, our recipe, meal-planning, grocery, pantry, family-sharing, import, and AI-assisted cooking app and related website (together, “Piper”).

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: your email address, a display name if you provide one, and the sign-in credentials managed by our authentication provider. We do not see your password in readable form.
  • Content you create: recipes, notes, ratings, favorites, meal plans, grocery and pantry lists, preferences (such as allergies, foods to avoid, household size, and cooking goals), and family-sharing settings.
  • Photos and text you submit: recipe photos, photos of recipe cards, and text you paste or share into Piper for import or AI features.
  • Support messages: information you send us when you contact us.

Information created by using Piper

  • Plan and entitlement status: whether you have Free, Pro, or Family access, how that access was granted (for example paid through Apple, beta, or complimentary), and any end date.
  • Usage counts: for example the number of AI actions used in a month and the number of recipes in your account, used to apply plan limits.
  • Family membership: if you join or create a Piper Family, the family’s member list (names and emails), membership dates, and the sharing settings described below.
  • Technical information: basic device, app-version, and error information reasonably needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot Piper, and timestamps such as when you last used the app.

Grocery price contributions

If you record a grocery price in Piper, we may store an anonymous community observation containing the item name, store name, ZIP code, category, aisle, price, and date. These observations are not linked to your account and do not include your name or email.

Purchase information

Apple processes App Store purchases. We may receive subscription status, product, and transaction identifiers needed to give you paid features. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, sync, and secure your account and recipes across your devices.
  • Import and organize recipes, and provide AI-assisted features you choose to use.
  • Enable optional Piper Family sharing.
  • Apply plan limits, manage subscriptions, and provide support.
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and security issues.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve Piper’s reliability and features.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. AI features and recipe processing

When you intentionally use Piper’s Chef, caption or social-post import, or recipe-card reading (including handwriting), the content needed for that request is sent to our server-side processing provider and AI models to produce the result. This may include the text you submitted, an existing recipe you are modifying, your allergy, avoidance, goal, and household preferences, or the photo you selected. We do not use your content to advertise to you. Photos sent for handwriting reading are processed to produce the result and are not saved by us as a separate photo library.

AI output can be inaccurate. Review ingredients, quantities, allergen information, temperatures, and food-safety guidance before relying on any recipe. Please do not submit sensitive personal information that a recipe request does not need.

Printed-text photo reading may be performed on your device.

4. Piper Family sharing

  • Recipes: members of your Piper Family can see recipes you have not marked private. You can mark any recipe private from its page. Family members can save a copy to their own account; they cannot edit or delete your recipe.
  • Meal planner: always private to each person.
  • Grocery list: private by default. The family owner may turn on one shared grocery list; if on, all family members can view, add, and change items on it.
  • The family owner can see members’ names and emails and can remove members. If you leave or are removed, you lose access to the family’s shared content.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers that help us run Piper, under their own terms and privacy practices. These currently include Google Firebase (authentication and database), Cloudflare (server-side processing, recipe search and import, and AI models), Cloudinary (recipe image hosting), Apple (App Store, TestFlight, and purchases), and GitHub (website and web-app hosting). We may add or change providers as Piper develops.
  • Other people, when you direct it, such as members of your Piper Family.
  • Legal and safety: to comply with law or legal process, enforce our Terms, or protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the public, or Piper.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

6. Retention

We keep account information and synced content while your account is active and as reasonably needed to provide Piper, resolve disputes, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. After deletion, limited logs or backups may persist for a short period before being removed. Anonymous grocery price observations are not tied to your account and may be retained.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit most information in Piper. For anything else, contact us.
  • Deletion: you can request deletion of your account and associated cloud data on our Delete Account page. A recipe that another family member separately saved to their own account is theirs and is not deleted when your account is deleted.
  • Sharing controls: manage recipe privacy and grocery sharing inside Piper.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel in your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting your Piper account does not cancel an App Store subscription.
  • State privacy rights: depending on where you live (for example Texas), you may have rights to confirm, access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal data, and to appeal a denial of your request. Email owner@upswingtx.com. We will respond as required by law.

8. Children

Piper is a general-audience service for people 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including access rules limiting who can read and change data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International processing

Piper is operated from the United States, and information may be processed and stored in the United States and in other places where our service providers operate.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Piper changes. We will post the new version here and update the effective date. If changes are material, we will also notify you in Piper where appropriate.
