Piper Support

We’re happy to help. Email owner@upswingtx.com and include your Piper account email, your device and iOS version, the Piper build number (bottom of Preferences), what you were trying to do, and the exact message you saw. Please never send passwords or payment-card details.

Common questions

A recipe won’t import from a website

Check your connection and try again. Some websites block automated readers or don’t publish a complete recipe; in that case, copy the recipe text and paste it into Add recipe, or enter it by hand.

Piper’s Chef or recipe import says it isn’t connected or has reached a limit

Make sure you’re online and signed in. AI features have monthly limits (shown in Preferences) and a shared daily capacity. If you’ve reached a limit, try again later or upgrade to Piper Pro.

My handwritten recipe card didn’t read correctly

Handwriting is less consistent than typed or printed cards. Use bright light, hold the phone straight above the card, fill the frame, and photograph one card at a time. Always check ingredients and amounts before saving.

How do I change or cancel my plan?

Open Piper → Preferences → Your plan. Subscriptions bought through Apple are changed or canceled in your Apple Account subscription settings. Beta or complimentary access can be changed by contacting us.

What happens to my recipes if I switch to Free?

Nothing is deleted. Free keeps 50 recipes active and moves the rest to a Pro archive. You choose which 50 stay active, and everything returns if you upgrade again.

How does Piper Family work?

One person buys Family and shares an invite code with up to four others. Everyone gets Pro features on their own account. Recipes you don’t mark private are visible to the family, meal planners are always private, and a shared grocery list is optional and controlled by the owner.

I see a family recipe twice

Piper hides unedited copies and combines identical imports automatically. If you edit a saved copy it appears as “your version.” Pull down to refresh the Family recipes list if something looks out of date.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete Account.

Food and allergy safety

Piper is a cooking aid. Always verify allergens, temperatures, and food-safety details yourself, especially for AI-generated recipes.
