Delete Your Piper Account

You can ask us to delete your Piper account and the cloud data connected to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email owner@upswingtx.com from the email address you use to sign in to Piper, with the subject Piper Account Deletion.
  2. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.
  3. We’ll delete your account and tell you when it’s done. We aim to finish within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in.
  • Your synced recipes, notes, meal plans, grocery and pantry lists, and preferences.
  • Your plan and usage records and your Piper Family membership. If you own a Family, its shared content is removed and members return to their own plans.

What may remain

  • Recipes that another family member saved to their own account belong to them and stay in their account.
  • Anonymous grocery price observations, which are not linked to you.
  • Limited backups or records we must keep for security or legal reasons, removed on a normal schedule.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel first in your Apple Account’s subscription settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) to avoid further charges.

Other privacy requests

To access or correct your information, or to exercise other privacy rights, email owner@upswingtx.com. See our Privacy Policy.
